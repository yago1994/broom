Safari Extension

Sweep clutter off any website. Hide the elements you don't want, plant something nice in the empty space, and your changes come back every visit.

Turn it on

  1. Click to open Safari Extensions.
    Nothing happened? Open it manually
    1. Open Safari, then click Safari in the macOS menu bar and choose Settings…
    2. Switch to the Extensions tab.
  2. Enable Broom in the sidebar.
  3. Set its access to Always Allow on Every Website, then confirm.
  4. Click the Broom icon in any tab to start sweeping.

Private by default

  • Nothing about your browsing leaves your Mac.
  • Rules live in Safari's local extension storage.
  • No accounts needed 🎉

Sweeping

  1. Tap the floating 🧹 in the bottom-right of any page, then pick 🧹 Broom.
  2. Left-click an element to sweep it away.
  3. Press Esc to exit.

Planting

  1. Open the floating broom, pick 🌱 Plant.
  2. Click any cleared spot to drop a plant.
  3. Hit Shuffle to swap it.

Restoring

  1. Open the floating broom, pick ♻️ Restore.
  2. Click the + on a hidden element to bring it back.
  3. ⚙️ Settings → Restore original wipes your changes on the whole site.
